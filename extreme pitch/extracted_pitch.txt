[PHILOSOPHY] WHAT IS THE PHILOSOPHY OF EXTREME PITCH?
"Question the project before the world questions it."Extreme Pitch is NOT created to judge or fail you. Its goal is to help your team find weak assumptions, security bugs, scaling bottlenecks, and UX issues on paper BEFORE your external college examiners or real users find them!

PART 1: HOW EXTREME PITCH WORKS (SIMPLE GUIDE)
Forget long academic theory. Extreme Pitch is a 3-person structured review game. You sit with your team or class, pick roles, ask tough questions, and log decisions so your project gets 10x better.
1.1 The 3-Participant Rotation System
You need exactly 3 participants: Person A, Person B, and Person C. In each round, 1 person defends their project (Product Owner), while the other 2 challenge them (Challengers).

1.2 Stakeholder Cards & Core Rules
• Stakeholder Cards belong to YOU, not the product: Before starting, each person picks 3 to 5 roles (e.g., Security Engineer, Investor, Database Admin, QA Tester, UI Designer). You keep these cards for the whole session!
• The Hidden Role Rule: The Product Owner does NOT know which role card you are using until you ask your question! Start your question by revealing your role: 'I am asking as a Security Engineer...'
• The Role Retirement Rule: You can use a specific card against a project only ONCE. Once 'Investor' challenges Project Zenith, it is retired for Zenith (but you can use it against another project in the next round).
• Concept-Level Technical Depth: You don't need to write code or open IDEs. Discuss logic, databases, security, cloud, monetization, scaling, and privacy at a high technical level.
• Fast & Simple Logging: The Facilitator records short signal logs: Question, Answer, Agreed Decision, Priority (P1/P2), and Status Badge (ACCEPTED / NEEDS IMPROVEMENT / REJECTED / RESEARCH REQUIRED). Never write long essays!

Section 2: Product Overview (Filled Example)
Product Summary
Zenith is a mobile PWA that uses lightweight AI models to triage emergency room patients in rural clinics and syncs patient records back to hospital servers even during internet outages.
Target Users & Market
Rural hospital emergency clinics, triage nurses, and medical staff in low-bandwidth areas.
Tech Stack & Architecture
React PWA (Frontend), Go microservices + NATS event bus (Backend), PostgreSQL + Edge SQLite (Database), Local XGBoost / Llama-3 (AI Triage).
Key Differentiator
Sub-second offline patient intake with automatic background data sync upon network reconnect.
